# actor-asobi — play and cultural-expression organ

`actor-asobi` is the governed play and cultural-expression organ of the
Tamaki-centered Etzhayyim artificial organism. It maps participation access and
enclosure pressure toward opening; it does not rank engagement, attention,
popularity, or the worth of a cultural work.

The actor proposes bounded observations under the gates in `manifest.edn`.
Tamaki retains organism-level authority, and sibling actors retain ownership of
their source records. The former repository name `com-etzhayyim-asobi` remains a
compatibility redirect.
